How this stays current

A GitHub Action runs every morning: it refreshes destination photos from Wikipedia, pulls public travel feeds, and rotates the daily selection. No API keys and no tracking. If a source is down, yesterday's data is kept rather than showing you nothing.

Sources & credits

Photos and summaries from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons under their respective licences. Featured picks from Wikivoyage (CC BY-SA). Outbound links to maps, flights and accommodation are plain searches — no affiliate tracking.

Use it properly

Visa, tax and border rules change without notice, and prices are rough guides that move. Confirm with official sources for your nationality before booking. Destination picks are editorial and never paid for — advertising is labelled and kept separate.
